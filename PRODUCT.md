# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who have already bought a product or subscribed to a service and want to notice price drops, plan changes, or more expensive renewals while there is still time to act.

## Product Purpose

AfterPrice remembers the baseline of a purchase or subscription, compares it with later information, records the evidence, makes timing visible, and points to a practical next action. Success means a person can understand what changed after purchase and decide whether to review, claim, change, keep, or dismiss it.

## Positioning

AfterPrice is organised around change after purchase: BASELINE -> CHANGE -> EVIDENCE -> DEADLINE -> ACTION -> RESOLUTION. It is not a budgeting tool, bank dashboard, coupon site, price-comparison site, or generic subscription manager.

## Operating Context

The current product is an authenticated Supabase-backed monitoring application. Shared catalogue identities are bootstrapped for cold-start search; private purchases, subscriptions and alerts begin empty and are created from the signed-in account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Manual purchase and subscription entry are supported.
- Purchase prices, return windows, subscription renewals, notification preferences and supported observations are stored in Supabase.
- Claim submission, retailer account access, bank connection and unsupported provider checks remain manual.
- The product must distinguish potential money from confirmed money and label illustrative public examples clearly.
- Existing public marketing routes remain functional and the authenticated app is protected by Supabase Auth.

## Brand Commitments

The business name is AfterPrice. Use AfterPrice consistently in user-facing copy, metadata, navigation, accessibility labels, and demo language. The voice is concrete and specific. Avoid unsupported promises, hype, and generic SaaS language.

## Evidence on Hand

Eight shared featured product identities and five shared subscription services are bootstrapped by the catalogue migration. There are no confirmed customer logos, testimonials, benchmarks, or external proof assets supplied for use in marketing.

## Product Principles

- Show the baseline before describing the change.
- Make evidence and timing visible beside the difference.
- Give the user one realistic next action.
- Treat potential recovery as potential until a real outcome is recorded.
- Keep automation claims honest and capability-specific.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals: keyboard access, visible focus, semantic headings and controls, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and usable layouts at mobile and desktop widths.
